What this scores, and why

The instinct is to sort wallets by profit and copy the top one. That fails for a specific, measurable reason: a profit leaderboard is a sorted in-sample pool. It ranks people by an outcome that was partly luck, and rank on such a pool carries no forward information.

Two real wallets, both measured from the public API:

Theo4 #1 all-time at $22.05M. Also: 38% of it from one position, only 4 of 23 months positive, $5.39M peak-to-trough, and dormant since Feb 2026. A lottery ticket that hit.
RN1 $12.5M across 4.7M fills, 100% positive months — statistically real. And 39% of profit is rebates and rewards, at a 2.1% edge per dollar. A market maker. You cannot follow someone into a quote.

So the board scores four things instead of profit:

Net / MaxDD
Profit per dollar of peak-to-trough pain. Scale-invariant, so it compares across bankrolls. Drawdown is measured on position_pnl — mark-inclusive — because a realized-only curve hides the pain of an open position that is 60% under until the day it closes.
Concentration
Share of lifetime profit from the single best position. High means the record is one bet, not a process.
Program income share
Maker rebates, liquidity rewards, referrals. Real money that accrues to market-making behaviour, and none of it transfers to a copier.
Edge per dollar traded
Above ~20% is a concentrated directional bettor; below ~3% on huge volume is a spread grinder. Copyable edge lives in between.

Two traps this had to be hardened against

The curve doesn't start at zero. One wallet's equity series opens at −$176,289. Seeding the high-water mark at the first observation treats "already six figures down" as the peak, erasing the entire inception drawdown and reporting a riskless-looking MaxDD = $0. The curve is now seeded at zero.

Some curves are fake. The API sometimes back-fills a flat line: one wallet's 93-point history was a single value repeated 93 times. A flat line has zero variance, therefore zero drawdown, therefore an infinite Net/DD — which would sort it straight to the top of a risk-first ranking. Wallets whose curve moves fewer than 10 times are marked INSUFFICIENT and refused a rank. Without that gate, the top of this board was four wallets whose drawdown was simply unmeasured.

What this deliberately does not do

It does not tell you to copy anyone, and it has no execution code — no keys, no orders, nothing that can place a trade. A high rank here means "worth forward-testing", not "worth money". The honest next step is to paper-track a shortlist forward and see whether rank survives out of sample. In a comparable study on futures configs, it did not.
