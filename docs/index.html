Topic leaderboards

Ranked by realized profit within the selected topic, among screened whales. Each whale's 200 most recent closed positions — a recent sample, not lifetime topic performance, and not picked by result. Open losses are not included. Topic labels are inferred from market text.

How the screen and specialist badges work At least 20 distinct closed markets, positive sampled profit, results across at least 3 closing months, 60% of those months positive, and no single market providing over 35% of gross winning profit. A specialist also has at least 60% of sampled cost in this topic. These are screening rules, not proof of future skill.

      Followability & holding profiles

      Followability checks whether $25 can still be bought within 2¢ of the whale’s fill, one minute after this browser sees a fresh live buy. Only followed whales are sampled, at most once per whale every 5 minutes. Keep this page open. The score requires 10 measured checks; failed checks are reported separately. Displayed depth is not a guaranteed fill, and fees are excluded.

      Observation spans are not exact holding times: gaps can hide close/reopen cycles. Quick trading and holding until settlement require entry and exit-reason history that the current snapshots cannot establish.
