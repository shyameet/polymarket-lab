What the proven whales did each day, and whether copying them would have paid

Only whales rated Worth following — not Promising, not Risky. A day is an India-time day (00:00–24:00 IST). Every buy of one outcome on one day is one entry, at its average price; smaller than $100 is left out as noise. A later sell closes the oldest entry first.

Won / lost counts only entries that are finished: sold, or the market settled. Open ones are shown at the last price and never counted. Parlays cannot be priced, so they are listed but not scored.

Price baseline is the chance the market itself gave: someone with no skill who pays 80¢ still wins about 80% of the time (a sell before the end counts as a coin flip). A whale only shows skill where the win rate beats it.

Per $100 copied uses the whale's own price and follows their exit, with taker fees charged. It is a ceiling: a real copy arrives later and pays more. Today and the two days before keep updating as markets settle; older days stay as last written.

Who makes money in each topic — from recent settled bets, not picked by result
Ranked by realized profit within the selected topic, among screened whales. Each whale's most recent settled positions — sold, redeemed, or resolved at $0 and never redeemed — up to 200. A recent sample, not lifetime topic performance, and not picked by result. Losses on positions still open are not included. Topic labels are inferred from market text.
How the screen and specialist badges work
At least 20 distinct settled markets, positive sampled profit, results across at least 3 months, 60% of those months positive, and no single market providing over 35% of gross winning profit. A specialist also has at least 60% of sampled cost in this topic. These are screening rules, not proof of future skill.

      Followability & holding profiles

      Could you still buy near the whale's price a minute later?
      Followability checks whether $25 can still be bought within 2¢ of the whale’s fill, one minute after this browser sees a fresh live buy. Only followed whales are sampled, at most once per whale every 5 minutes. Keep this page open. The score requires 10 measured checks; failed checks are reported separately. Displayed depth is not a guaranteed fill, and fees are excluded. Observation spans are not exact holding times: gaps can hide close/reopen cycles.
